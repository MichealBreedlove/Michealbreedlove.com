Adaptive Routing

A health-aware scheduler scores every job against node health, role fitness, and observed success rate. Routing policy tunes itself as execution history accumulates — no manual rebalancing.

Distributed Immutable State

Operational knowledge is stored as versioned plain text on snapshot-protected storage, with nightly curation that promotes durable facts and archives noise. No shared database, no lock contention, no single point of failure.

Self-Healing

A runtime watchdog detects degradation, quarantines corrupt state, and restores from verified backups automatically. Preflight guards refuse to start on inconsistent state.

Disaster Recovery

A portable recovery bundle rebuilds the entire control plane on clean infrastructure. Monthly sandboxed DR drills prove the bundle restores without touching production state.

Autonomous Operations

A rule engine turns health signals into remediation tasks. Safe actions execute automatically; destructive or high-impact actions are held in an approval queue, bounding the blast radius of automation.

Durable Job Queue

Database-free work queue on shared storage with dispatch tracking, retry policy, stale-lease detection, and failure isolation. Crash recovery is a property of the design, not a bolt-on.
